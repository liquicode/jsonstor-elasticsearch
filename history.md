# Project History


v0.2.0 (current)
---------------------------------------------------------------------

***First release.***

The adapter for Elasticsearch and OpenSearch, translating a criteria into a query. Tested on
  Elasticsearch 7.17, 8.19 and 9.5 and OpenSearch 1.3, 2.19 and 3.8.

- Built on `@liquicode/jsonstor` 0.2.0 and `@liquicode/jsongin` 0.2.0. A criteria the engine
  refuses is refused before the storage acts on it.
- Declares Node.js `>=18.0.0` in `engines`.
